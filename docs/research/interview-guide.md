# User Interview Guide

**Goal:** Validate that people who take multiple supplements (a) worry about interactions or redundancy, (b) don't have a good way to check, and (c) are open to lifestyle-first guidance.

**Recruit:** 8–10 adults taking ≥3 supplements. Aim for a mix of ages, with ≥3 participants also on a prescription medication. Exclude anyone you work with.

**Privacy:** Don't record names or identifying health details. Notes go in this folder under `interviews/` with a participant code only (P01, P02…), and summaries only.

## Script (30 min)

### Warm-up (3 min)
1. What do you do day to day, and how do you think about your health?

### Current stack (8 min)
2. Walk me through every supplement you take right now. (Note them.)
3. For each one: when did you start, and why? Where did you hear about it?
4. Have you ever stopped one? Why?

### Checking and trust (8 min)
5. Have you ever wondered whether two things you take might conflict? What did you do?
6. Does your doctor or pharmacist know everything you take? Why or why not?
7. Where do you go when you want to know if a supplement *works*? How much do you trust it?

### Lifestyle vs. supplement (6 min)
8. Think of the last supplement you added. Was there a non-supplement way to get the same benefit? Did you consider it?
9. *Show two copy variants*: (A) "Try these 3 foods first" vs. (B) "Here's the supplement." Which would you prefer to see, and why?

### Wrap-up (5 min)
10. If a tool could check your whole stack, what would it have to do for you to trust it?
11. Would you bring a printed summary to your doctor?
