# Competitor Scan

> 🟡 In progress. Descriptions are based on publicly available product positioning and should be re-checked before external use, because products change.

## Landscape by category

| Category | Examples | What they do well | Where they fall short for our user |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Drug-interaction checkers** | Consumer drug-reference and pharmacy sites (e.g. Drugs.com, Medscape interaction checkers) | Broad drug coverage, familiar and trusted | Built around drugs, with uneven supplement coverage. No "do you need this?" guidance or evidence grades |
| **Evidence databases (consumer)** | Examine | Deep, well-referenced research summaries | Organized by ingredient, not by stack. Interaction checking isn't the main job. Paid tiers |
| **Evidence databases (professional)** | Natural Medicines (TRC) | Comprehensive interaction and efficacy ratings | Written for clinicians, institutional pricing, not consumer-friendly |
| **Government and public resources** | NIH ODS fact sheets, NCCIH, MedlinePlus | Authoritative and free | Static pages one ingredient at a time, no stack view |
| **Personalized supplement brands** | Quiz-based subscription packs (several have launched and some have shut down) | Easy onboarding and personalization | The recommendation is the thing being sold. Lifestyle advice is secondary |
| **Nutrition trackers** | Cronometer, MyFitnessPal | Detailed food-nutrient data | Doesn't connect intake to supplement decisions or interactions |
| **General AI chatbots** | Various | Conversational, instant | Can invent facts and citations, and grounding isn't transparent |

## Positioning

```
                    Evidence transparency ↑
                              │
         Evidence DBs ●       │      ★ Supplement Stack
                              │
  Ingredient-centric ─────────┼───────── Stack-centric
                              │
         Gov't pages ●        │      ● Interaction checkers
                              │      ● Supplement brands (conflicted)
                              ↓
```

**Our wedge:** stack-centric, evidence-transparent, lifestyle-first, and no commercial conflict.
